\chapter*{Acknowledgements}

There are people without whom this dissertation simply wouldn't exist - and they deserve more than a page.
First, my sincere thanks go to my supervisor Dr Joey Lam, whose guidance throughout this process was, at times, the only thing keeping the work on track. The questions they pushed me to ask - and the ones they pushed me to abandon - shaped this research in ways I didn't always appreciate at the moment, but do now. Their door was rarely closed, and that mattered more than I expected it to.
I also owe a great deal to my family. They didn't always understand what I was working on, and honestly, that was fine - what they gave me instead was steadiness. Through the late nights and the stretches where progress felt impossibly slow, their support was consistent and, at times, quietly essential. I couldn't have finished this without them.
